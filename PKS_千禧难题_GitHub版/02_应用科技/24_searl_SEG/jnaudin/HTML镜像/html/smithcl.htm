The Smith Coil

A part of the TEP Project

created on 09/15/97 - JLN Labs - last update on 09/16/97

Notes :

The energy absorption causes a decrease or "dip" in the oscillator circuit, measured by the DC microammeter of the grid-dip meter.

The maximum energy absorbed by the coil is at 36.80 MHz ( dip = 0.40 )
