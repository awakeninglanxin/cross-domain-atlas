January 25, 2013 - TESTS02E01 : Following the latest TEST#17 about the production of HHO gaz with the GEGENE, I have continued the tests with the Stanley Meyer's Water Fuel Cell (WFC). The TEST#17 has demonstrated that High Voltage Nano Pulses at High Frequency produced by the GEGENE connected accross an electrolysis cell are able to produce very quickly a great amount of HHO gaz.

According to these encouraging tests, I have reconducted my experiments done about the Stanley Meyer's WFC from June 2008. I have replaced the VIC (Voltage Intensifier Circuit), a replication of the S. Meyer's circuit, by the GEGENE and used my old Water Fuel Cell with some new update.

Here the setup of the TEST-S02E01 :

Here is the Water Fuel Cell, the most important thing is that the WFC uses only ORDINARY TAP WATER without need of ELECTROLYTE for working properly. The stainless steel electrodes are the main parts of a water capacitor.

The High Frequency is rectified via an ultra fast HFA25PB60 diode so as to produce High Frequency DC pulses accross the WFC.
